# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (unreleased)

First version.

- Assets of every class: stocks, FIIs, ETFs, BDRs, fixed income, funds, crypto,
  cash, real estate and business stakes, in BRL or USD.
- Buys and sales with fees, average cost and realized gain.
- Valuations for real estate and business stakes, held as an ownership fraction.
- Income records: dividends, JCP, rent, profit distributions and interest.
- Automatic quotes from brapi.dev (B3) and Finnhub (US), and the official
  USD/BRL PTAX rate from the Banco Central do Brasil.
- Local web app with net worth, allocation by class, type and currency, and a
  page per asset.
- Command line: `serve`, `refresh`, `summary` and `demo`.
